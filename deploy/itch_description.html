⚔️ SAMURAI EDGE: BAKUMATSU

"One strike. One kill. No second chances."

Samurai Edge: Bakumatsu is a lethal, tactical isometric dueling game rendered in crisp 3D Voxel Art and Faceted Anime Architecture. Inspired by the unforgiving tension of timeless classics like Bushido Blade, Akira Kurosawa’s samurai cinema (Sanjuro, Yojimbo), and vintage PS1 fighters, this is a game where patience, spacing, and nerves of steel decide who walks away alive.

Forget 50-hit air juggle combos and spongy health bars: in Samurai Edge, a single clean katana cut, well-placed arrow, or point-blank arquebus blast is fatal. Every step is a calculated gamble; every swing carries the weight of life and death.

✨ KEY FEATURES

  • ⚡ Bushido Lethal Combat (1-Hit Kill): True one-strike lethality where tension and spacing reign supreme. (Only the legendary dual-blade master Miyamoto Musashi can endure up to 3 strikes through his iron defensive posture).
  • 🎥 Dynamic Bushido Duel Camera: A reactive isometric camera system that pulls in tight for breath-stealing standoffs when warriors circle at close quarters, and smoothly pans out to capture leaping aerial strikes and projectile trajectories.
  • 🩸 Cinematic Kurosawa Arterial Fatalities: Vintage samurai cinema violence featuring dramatic hitstop freezes, stark black-and-white contrast flashes, delayed collapses, arterial blood fountains, and volumetric 3D dismemberment.
  • ⚔️ Blade Clash QTE System (Tsubazeriai): When equal attacks collide, weapons lock in a shower of sparks! Rapidly mash the attack button to overpower your opponent and break their guard.
  • 🥋 The 24-Warrior Roster: A massive roster of 24 lethal warriors spanning feudal Japan, ancient Heian sorcery, Ainu frontiers, and 19th-century Western duelists.
  • 🏯 22+ Dynamic Living Arenas: Fight across sliceable bamboo groves, burning Kyoto avenues with runaway carriages, moonlit rooftops, Prussian cemeteries, and revolving Kabuki stages.
  • 👹 Arcade Mode & Boss Duel: Climb a 12-round trial against rival swordsmen, ninja ambushes, and endurance trials, culminating in a showdown against the mythical Boss Oni, followed by an animated ending credits slideshow.
  • 🎛️ Interactive Media & Settings Hub: A full Sumi-e styled hub featuring a 24-track Jukebox with synchronized karaoke lyrics sweep, an Art Gallery showcasing original concept art, customizable keybindings, and dynamic AI difficulty.
  • 🎮 Universal Controls & Gamepad Support: Full support for Xbox, PlayStation DualShock/DualSense, Nintendo Switch, generic arcade sticks, keyboard, and mobile touch controls with vector badges.
  • 🌐 Fully Bilingual (EN / PT-BR): Seamlessly switch between English and Brazilian Portuguese at any moment, complete with an in-game F1 martial arts manual.
  • 🍏 Apple Developer Signed: Built with Apple Hardened Runtime for effortless, gatekeeper-free play on macOS.

Each combatant possesses a bespoke moveset, distinct weapon reach, movement cadence, and cinematic finishers:

  • 🥷 Hanzo (Iga Shinobi): High agility, agile wall jumps, evasive rolling recovery, and lethal throwing kunai that embed into the terrain.
  • 🤠 Joe & Yamato (Western Ninjutsu): Acrobatic 360° tumbling flips, rapid-fire stagger shurikens, live banjo strumming, and a tactical war hound that leaps and pins foes.

  • 🎯 Teppo (Tanegashima Matchlock): Devastating long-range firearms with reloadable powder keg mechanics, leaping evasions, and rifle-butt melee strikes.
  • ⛓️ Murasaki (Kusarigama Specialist): Absolute strike precedence; sweeping chain sickles that disarm and drag opponents across the arena.
  • 💨 Kasumi (Art of Smoke & Explosives): High-arc ceramic bombs that bypass obstacles, poisonous blinding smoke clouds, and decoy substitutions.
  • 🎭 Okuni (Kabuki Poison Dancer): Iron war-fan (Tessen) deflections and theatrical venom breath triggering a lethal 10-second countdown.
  • 🏹 Tomoe (Sacred Kyudo): Arena-wide longbow range with armor-piercing arrows and tethered wire shots for rapid repositioning.
  • 🏴‍☠️ Anne (Buccaneer Cutlass): 180° sweeping heavy cutlass slashes and blinding black powder pocket flares.
  • 🤺 Julie (Royal Musketeer): Fencing rapier lunges (Flèche) and defensive cloak deflections backed by an off-hand flintlock pistol.
  • 🔱 Benkei (The Naginata Guardian): Colossal polearm reach, hyper-armor forward thrusts, and crushing overhead sweeps.
  • 🪕 O-Rin (The Blind Minstrel): Acoustic shockwaves channeled through her Biwa/Shamisen that slice through incoming projectiles.
  • 🌾 Goro (Rebel Farmer): Lethal farming hoe with unpredictable low hooks and an earth-shattering ground stomp.
  • 🦯 Ichi (The Blind Wanderer): Sound-based reactionary iai strikes that unsheathe and cleave before footsteps are heard.
  • 💀 Valerius (Prussian Necromancer): Precision fencing thrusts paired with dark soul-drain projectiles and spectral skull summons.
  • ☯️ Seimei (Heian Onmyoji Master): Five-element Yin-Yang paper talismans managed through a dynamic UI rondel.
  • 🏔️ Daiki (Bo Staff Pilgrim): 360° spinning staff flourishes, pole vaults over hazards, and sweeping low knockdowns.
  • 🦅 Aoi (Ainu Huntress): Twin hand-sickles backed by a hunting golden eagle that dives to harass and blind opponents.
  • 🤼 Raiden (Sumo Yokozuna): Unstoppable 2-hit Harite palm thrusts and shockwave-inducing Dohyo ring stomps.

  • 🗡️ Ren (Ronin Vagabond): Raw, unorthodox street-fighting katana cuts and deceptive off-beat feints.

🏯 22+ BATTLEFIELDS & HAZARDS

Every duel takes place on historically inspired, mechanically interactive terrain:

  1. 🎋 Bamboo Forest: Dense sliceable bamboo thickets and a zen reflecting pool that slows grounded movement.
  2. 🔥 Kyoto Bakumatsu: A wide burning avenue plagued by runaway royal carriages charging at lethal speed and falling roof tiles.
  3. 🌊 Ganryū Island: Tidal sands with dynamic ocean surges, sandbars, and narrow wooden planks.
  4. 🏯 Iga Rooftops: Moonlit temple roofs with deadly drop-offs requiring agile leaps across timber beams.
  5. 🏴‍☠️ Pirate Deck: Rolling galleon deck tilting with ocean waves and deck cannons that can be ignited.
  6. ⛓️ Cave of Shadows: Deep caverns with swinging iron pendulums and indestructible Jizo statues for cover.
  7. 🌫️ Temple in the Mist: Heavy volumetric mist obscuring sightlines and mortar firework traps.
  8. ⛺ Forest Camp: Hidden hunter snare traps and destructible wooden supply crates.
  9. 💥 Nagashino Field: Muddy trenches littered with reactive explosive gunpowder barrels.
  10. 🎭 Kabuki Stage: A rotating circular stage floor with fragile paper screen partitions.
  11. ⛩️ Mountain Shrine: Sacred Torii gates and a giant temple bell that dispels smoke and poison when struck.
  12. 👑 Baroque Court: Formal royal gardens featuring sculpted hedges, marble walkways, and fountains.
  13. 🌉 Gojo Bridge: High stone railings over a deep river gorge.
  14. 🏮 Yoshiwara Alley: A vibrant night pleasure district with breakable market stalls.
  15. 🌾 Terraced Rice Paddies: Cascading water terraces that splash and dampen fast dashes.
  16. 🌧️ Rain Mill: Heavy downpours, dynamic lightning strikes, and a rotating water wheel.
  17. ⚰️ Gothic Cemetery: Crumbling Prussian gravestones wrapped in thick autumn fog.
  18. ⛩️ Heian Shrine: Vermilion colonnades and floating paper talismans.
  19. 🌲 Sacred Pines: Giant alpine evergreens with mountain mist and steep slopes.
  20. ❄️ Ezo Tundra: Biting northern blizzards and knee-deep snowdrifts.
  21. ⭕ Sacred Dohyo: The sanctified clay sumo ring ringed by sacred rice straw rope.
  22. ⚓ Dejima Warehouse: The historic Dutch trade post stacked high with spice crates and docking ropes.
