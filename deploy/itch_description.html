⚔️ SAMURAI EDGE: BAKUMATSU

"One strike. One kill. No second chances."

Samurai Edge Demake is an intense, tactical isometric lethal dueling game rendered in 3D Voxel Art and HD-2D. Inspired by the unforgiving realism of timeless classics like Bushido Blade, Akira Kurosawa’s samurai cinema (Sanjuro, Yojimbo), and retro demake aesthetics.

Forget 50-hit air juggle combos and bloated health bars: in Samurai Edge, a single clean katana cut, well-placed arrow, or point-blank arquebus blast is instantly fatal. Every approach requires patience, cold nerve, and millimeter spacing.

✨ KEY FEATURES

  • ⚡ Bushido Lethal Combat (1-Hit Kill): True one-strike lethality where tension and spacing reign supreme. (Only the master Musashi can withstand up to 3 blows through dual-blade endurance).
  • 🩸 Cinematic Kurosawa Noir Violence: Dramatic hitstop freeze, stark high-contrast monochrome flashes, delayed death collapses, persistent arena bloodstains, and volumetric 3D voxel dismemberment.
  • ⚔️ Blade Clash QTE System: When strikes meet at equal priority, blades lock together! Rapidly mash attack to overpower your foe and stagger them backwards.
  • 🥋 12 Balanced Warriors: 6 lethal women and 6 master swordsmen spanning traditional Iaijutsu, Niten Ichi-ryū, Shinobi clans, Kabuki poison dancers, and Western musketeers.
  • 🗺️ 12 Dynamic Themed Arenas: From the sliceable bamboo thickets of the Sacred Forest and burning Kyoto streets with runaway carriages, to storm-battered pirate decks and revolving Kabuki stages.
  • 🤖 Adaptive AI & Local Versus: Deep tactical AI with character-specific strategies (spacing, bamboo ambushes, parry baiting) + instant 2-Player couch multiplayer.
  • 🎮 Full Controller & Touch Support: Controller-aware on-screen button prompts (PlayStation, Xbox, Switch) and intuitive mobile virtual controls.
  • 🌐 Fully Bilingual: Switch instantly between English (EN) and Português (PT-BR) in-game (F1 interactive strategy manual included).

🥋 THE 12 WARRIORS

  • Kenshi (Iaijutsu & Shukuchi): Lightning quickdraw lunge; divine afterimage dash.
  • Musashi (Niten Ichi-ryū - Dual Blades): 3 HP resilience; extended combo reach; projectile parry posture.
  • Hanzo (Ninjutsu & Kunai): Maximum sprint speed; fatal throw kunai (must be retrieved if missed).
  • Joe & Doberman (Western Ninjutsu & Tactical Dog): High-speed stun shuriken paired with a lethal charging war hound.
  • Saitou (Shinsengumi - Gatotsu): Supersonic accelerating thrust (Shinsen) + point-blank Zeroshiki.
  • Teppo (Tanegashima Matchlock): Fatal matchlock bullets; requires reloading from powder kegs.
  • Murasaki (Kusarigama - Sickle & Chain): Absolute Precedence (wins simultaneous trades); hook & pull.
  • Kasumi (Art of Smoke & Bombs): 3D arcing ceramic bombs over obstacles + blinding slow smoke.
  • Okuni (Kabuki Dance & Venom): Toxic breath starting a fatal 10-second death countdown!
  • Tomoe (Sacred Kyudo - Yumi Bow): Longest range in the arena; grappling string arrow for quick escapes.
  • Anne (Buccaneer Cutlass): Sweeping 180° cutlass arcs + close-quarters blinding black powder.
  • Julie (Royal Guard - Foil & Cloak): Rapid Flèche lunges + defensive silk cloak riposte with flintlock pistol.

🗺️ THE 12 ARENAS & ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS

  1. 🎋 Bamboo Forest: Cuttable bamboo stalks and 45% slowing water lake.
  2. 🔥 Kyoto Bakumatsu: Lethal runaway carriage crossing at 14.0 tiles/s and falling roof debris.
  3. 🌊 Ganryū Island: Low-tide duel with rising water surges and plank bridges.
  4. 🏯 Iga Rooftops: Lethal rooftop pits requiring dashes or leaps across beams.
  5. 🏴‍☠️ Storm Pirate Deck: Ship rolling tilts fighters; light cannons to sweep the deck.
  6. ⛓️ Cave of Shadows: Swiveling iron chain pendulums; Jizo statues block shots.
  7. 🌫️ Temple in the Mist: Dense volumetric fog and explosive firework mortar circles.
  8. ⛺ Forest Camp: Hidden stun snare traps and wooden supply crates for cover.
  9. 💥 Nagashino Field: Slow mud and reactive explosive powder kegs.
  10. 🎭 Kabuki Stage: Revolving stage disc and projectile-stopping folding screens.
  11. ⛩️ Mountain Shrine: Sacred Torii gates; strike the giant bell to dispel poison/smoke.
  12. 👑 Baroque Court: Checkered marble palace gardens with hedge and fountain cover.

🎮 CONTROLS

  • Player 1: WASD to Move | E to Attack | R for Special/Utility | T to Dodge | C Settings | F1 Help Manual
  • Player 2: Arrow Keys to Move | U to Attack | I for Special/Utility | O to Dodge
  • Gamepads: Plug-and-play support for Xbox, PlayStation DualShock/DualSense, and generic USB arcade sticks.
  • Mobile: Dynamic floating virtual analog stick + multi-touch action buttons.
