Every single warrior received their authentic home ground with sliceable objects, interactive terrain, and weather particles:

  • 🌉 Gojo Bridge: Narrow river crossing with historic stone railings.
  • 🏮 Yoshiwara Alley: Vibrant red-lantern pleasure district with breakable wooden stalls.
  • 🌾 Terraced Rice Paddies: Sloping flooded terraces that kick up water sprays and slow down grounded dashes.

  • ⚰️ Gothic Cemetery: Atmospheric Prussian graveyard with crumbling tombstones and autumn fog.
  • ⛩️ Heian Shrine: Sacred ancient shrine with vermilion columns and floating paper wards.
  • 🌲 Sacred Pines: Towering alpine evergreens with drifting mountain mist.
  • ❄️ Ezo Tundra: Biting northern blizzards with knee-deep powder snow.

  • ⚓ Dejima Warehouse: Historic Dutch trading post packed with spice crates and harbor ropes.

🎥 3. Phase 1: Dynamic Cinematic Camera (Bushido Stand-Off System)

The isometric view is now fully reactive and cinematic:

  • 🔍 Bushido Standoff Zoom: As fighters circle each other at close range, the camera dynamically pushes in tight for dramatic, high-tension standoffs. When backing away, it smoothly pulls out to reveal the surrounding arena.
  • 📐 Ballistic Projectile & Jump Tracking: High-velocity matchlock rounds, throwing kunai, arrows, and aerial leaps pull the camera frame dynamically without clipping arena boundaries.

🩸 4. Phase 2: Master 3D Character Overhauls & Fatalities

We initiated our comprehensive fighter overhaul pipeline (featuring 2x SSAA, 3D low-poly faceted anime facial architecture, and authentic martial arts rigging):

That means characters from now on won't have tearing edges or static frame animations! The aim is to make the game fluid and dynamic. With the roster doubled, I am rebalancing moves and creating new ones so every warrior feels truly unique. Characters now have faces and expressive eyes, reminiscent of the classic PS1 era!

So far, I've overhauled Kenshi, Saitou, Musashi, Hanzo, Joe, and Raiden. I'm so pleased with the results that I wanted to release this alpha version so you can play with them while I work on the rest!

  • ⚡ Kenshi (Iaijutsu Master): Authentic Iai Koiguchi-kiri (thumb unlatch before drawing), 3-phase aerial Ryuu Tsui Sen downward dive, and Shukuchi cancel into 360° backflip roll with knee tuck. Dual cinematic fatalities: Decapitation & Noto Sheathing (blade sheathed in slow motion while the severed head flies with arterial blood spray) and Mid-Air Bisecting.
  • ⚔️ Miyamoto Musashi (Niten Ichi-ryū): Dynamic Painter's Algorithm depth sorting for dual katanas and scabbards (cleanly occluded behind kimono when facing away). Mae-ukemi forward dive recovery roll and projectile parry stance. Overhauled Finisher A (Dual-Impale X-Spread) cleanly bisecting the rival down the spine.
  • 🥷 Hanzo (Iga Shinobi): Seam-free shinobi cowl and face mask geometry. Wall jumps, fluid rolling recovery, and fixed kunai physics (sticks into the ground on impact, properly respecting Musashi's 3 HP resilience).
  • 🤠 Joe & Yamato (Western Ninjutsu): Continuous 360° tumbling flic-flac acrobatics in the facing direction. Tactical war hound Yamato with smart parabolic trajectory, boosted pursuit speed, and micro-stun on bite. Rapid-fire shurikens with stagger properties. Animated victory celebration: Stetson hat tip, seated posture, and live acoustic banjo strumming (with dedicated banjo audio!). Brand-new Finisher B (Triple Shuriken Dismemberment): 3 flying shurikens severing both arms and the head simultaneously.
  • 🐺 Saitou Hajime (The Wolf of Mibu): Permanent Gatotsu Kamae stance with drawn blade held in left-hand spear grip. Accelerating supersonic Gatotsu charge and point-blank Zeroshiki. Overhauled Finisher A (Heart Impale & Backwards Blood Geyser): Hoists the victim overhead with a single arm while a massive arterial blood fountain erupts from their back!
  • 🤼 Raiden (Sumo Yokozuna): Full low-poly anatomy with authentic mawashi belt and topknot. 2-hit Harite palm combo and shockwave Do-hyo stomp.
