# 🎵 Prompts de Geração Musical no Gemini (BGM das Arenas)

Este documento registra os prompts especializados para alimentar ferramentas de geração musical com IA (como **Gemini MusicLM / Lyria**, Suno, Udio e similares) para a trilha sonora (BGM) das arenas do jogo.

A estética geral das faixas segue o padrão **90s Neo-Geo / SNK Feudal Arcade** (estilo *Samurai Shodown*, *The Last Blade* e *Ninja Master's*), unindo instrumentação acústica tradicional japonesa/asiática com percussão visceral de batalha, sintetizadores cinemáticos e mixagem balanceada para duelos mortais de alta tensão.

### 1. Pátio dos Sinos Shaolin (`shaolin_temple`)
* **Personagem Principal**: Ren (Monge Shaolin)
* **Ambiente**: Mosteiro budista nas montanhas ao entardecer, cascalho de treino, sinos monumentais de bronze, colunas de fumaça de incenso e folhas douradas de ginkgo flutuando.
* **Clima Emocional**: Disciplina solene, serenidade marcial concentrada, poder espiritual iminente que explode em fúria controlada a cada golpe.

#### 🎙️ Prompt Gemini (English - High Fidelity MusicLM / Lyria):
```text
Title: Courtyard of the Sacred Bronze Bells
Genre: Traditional Feudal Asian Martial Soundtrack / Neo-Geo Arcade Battle Theme
Tempo: 118 BPM, 4/4 Time Signature, Key of D Dorian / Pentatonic D Minor
Instrumentation: Resonant low-frequency bronze temple bell (bonshō) resonating on the downbeat, brisk polyrhythmic Chinese tanggu and Japanese nagado taiko war drums, sharp bamboo clappers (hyoshigi), expressive bamboo flutes (dizi and shakuhachi) trading rapid ornamentation, deep resonant guzheng and koto arpeggios, subtle warm analog low-drone synthesizer.
Structure & Dynamics:
- Intro (0:00 - 0:12): A massive bronze bell tolls with long natural reverb, accompanied by soft swirling temple wind, solitary evocative dizi flute melody, building anticipation.
- Battle Groove (0:12 - 0:45): Fierce driving tanggu percussion enters with intricate martial rhythms; rapid guzheng plucking creates intense momentum; powerful soaring flute themes evoke ancient Shaolin combat forms.
- Climax / Duel Peak (0:45 - 1:15): Accelerating taiko roll, brassy suona accents with dramatic pentatonic tension, syncopated kiai-like percussive strikes, sudden tight stops mimicking lethal sword duels.
- Seamless Outro / Loop Tail: Smooth tail naturally resolving back into the opening bronze bell resonance for a seamless fighting game loop.
Mix & Texture: Warm analog vintage master, punchy low-end percussion, clear sparkling high flute harmonics, organic room acoustics of an open stone monastery courtyard. 90s SNK arcade fighting game aesthetic, evocative, honorable, adrenaline-charged.
```

#### 📜 Descrição Conceitual (Português):
* **Foco Rítmico**: Batidas secas e rápidas de Tanggu acompanhadas pelo eco grave e reverberante do Sino de Bronze que dita a gravidade do combate.
* **Arranjo Melódico**: Diálogo entre o Dizi (flauta transversal chinesa) e o Shakuhachi, transmitindo maestria kung fu e agilidade letal.

### 2. Jardim dos Lírios Carmim (`higanbana_garden`)
* **Personagem Principal**: Chiyo (Kunoichi das Duas Nodachis)
* **Ambiente**: Pântano noturno iluminado por névoa arroxeada e luar frio, campos infinitos de flores carmesim (Higanbana / Spider Lily), pontes de pedra escura e lanternas trêmulas.
* **Clima Emocional**: Ominoso, melancólico, sensual e fatal. Uma dança elegante da morte onde pétalas vermelhas são cortadas por lâminas duplas velozes.

#### 🎙️ Prompt Gemini (English - High Fidelity MusicLM / Lyria):
```text
Title: Dance of the Crimson Spider Lilies
Genre: Feudal Japanese Dark Ambient Battle / Kunoichi Theme / Neo-Geo Arcade OST
Tempo: 136 BPM, 4/4 Time Signature, Key of F# Minor (In-sen Japanese Scale)
Instrumentation: Virtuosic fast-paced Tsugaru shamisen with aggressive percussive plectrum strikes, dark trembling biwa chords, eerie high-register Shakuhachi and Shinobue flutes with breathy fluttering techniques, deep muted taiko drums, metallic tsuzumi shoulder drums, ominous sub-bass synth swells, distant wind chimes and fluttering cloth textures.
Structure & Dynamics:
- Intro (0:00 - 0:10): Hypnotic distant night wind with eerie dissonant shakuhachi breath noises; a single sorrowful biwa chord strikes as spider lily petals swirl.
- Danse Macabre (0:10 - 0:48): Sudden explosion into a lightning-fast, rhythmic Tsugaru shamisen riff in rapid sixteenth notes; urgent tsuzumi and taiko rhythms drive a stealthy, predatory combat cadence.
- Duel Tension (0:48 - 1:12): Complex polyrhythmic percussion breaks, dual shamisen counterpoints evoking two dancing nodachi blades slicing through the air, dark gothic string pads swelling beneath the traditional strings.
- Seamless Loop Tail: A breathless shamisen cadence that cuts cleanly into the opening nocturnal wind gust, designed for seamless infinite battle looping.
Mix & Texture: Crisp, razor-sharp string transients, shadowy stereo imaging with sweeping mist effects, midnight atmospheric reverb, aesthetic reminiscent of classic Samurai Shodown IV / The Last Blade 2.
```

#### 📜 Descrição Conceitual (Português):
* **Foco Rítmico**: Shamisen acelerado tocado com intensidade percussiva (banchi), emulando os giros contínuos das duas Nodachis em alta rotação.
* **Arranjo Melódico**: Escala japonesa In-sen sombria, intercalando momentos de suspense fantasmagórico com rajadas de velocidade cortante.

### 3. Campo de Nagashino - Visual Revamp (`nagashino_field`)
* **Personagem Principal**: Teppo (Atirador de Arcabuz)
* **Ambiente**: Campo de batalha devastado crepuscular, paliçadas de madeira queimadas, fumaça densa de pólvora, cinzas e brasas incandescentes sopradas pelo vento, estandartes rasgados dos clãs Oda e Takeda.
* **Clima Emocional**: Guerra sem trégua, tensão sufocante de fuzilaria iminente, peso histórico, terra encharcada de pólvora e sangue.

#### 🎙️ Prompt Gemini (English - High Fidelity MusicLM / Lyria):
```text
Title: Ash and Gunpowder at Nagashino
Genre: Epic Feudal Military War Theme / Gunpowder Era Japanese Battle March
Tempo: 112 BPM, 4/4 March Cadence, Key of E Minor / Pentatonic War Scale
Instrumentation: Resonant Horagai (conch horn) war calls, massive thunderous Oda clan war taiko ensemble (ōdaiko and shime-daiko), clattering wooden matchlock ramrods used as industrial percussion, tense rhythmic biwa, raw distorted shamisen bassline, mournful noh flute (nohkan) crying over the battlefield, deep rumbling brass drone.
Structure & Dynamics:
- Intro (0:00 - 0:14): An ominous deep Horagai conch horn echoes across smoke-choked hills; distant gunpowder rumble and snapping timber fires; low military drum roll begins.
- Battle March (0:14 - 0:52): Heavy, relentless martial cadence on massive taiko drums; aggressive syncopated biwa and biting shamisen riffs symbolize marching ashigaru rifle ranks; sharp sudden percussive snaps evoke firearm volleys.
- War Climax (0:52 - 1:18): Wall of thunderous drums reaches a crescendo; screaming nohkan flute adds piercing war agony; brass drones swell in tragic samurai military grandeur.
- Seamless Loop Tail: Low simmering drum rumble and windblown embers decaying gracefully into the initial war horn prelude.
Mix & Texture: Gritty, visceral, heavy analog presence with explosive mid-bass punch; dust and smoke ambiance in the background; aesthetic matching the raw intensity of 90s SNK military stages.
```

#### 📜 Descrição Conceitual (Português):
* **Foco Rítmico**: Marcha militar pesada e implacável com taikos gigantescos (Ōdaiko) e estalos secos de percussão militar que lembram tiros e recarga de arcabuzes.
* **Arranjo Melódico**: O chamado do Horagai (trompa de búzio) sobre acordes graves de Biwa e flauta Nohkan fúnebre, traduzindo o fim da era samurai tradicional diante da pólvora.

### 4. Gruta das Sombras - Visual Revamp (`shadow_cave`)
* **Personagem Principal**: Murasaki / Kasumi (Bruxa das Sombras / Mística)
* **Ambiente**: Caverna subterrânea ritualística, círculo magenta brilhante no piso de pedra, correntes de ferro suspensas oscilando, estátuas Jizo em roda, fogueiras místicas e névoa densa púrpura.
* **Clima Emocional**: Ocultismo esotérico, claustrofobia mágica, perigo ritualístico sobrenatural, encantamentos antigos ecoando na rocha úmida.

#### 🎙️ Prompt Gemini (English - High Fidelity MusicLM / Lyria):
```text
Title: Ritual in the Obsidian Cavern
Genre: Occult Feudal Japanese Dark Ambient / Ritualistic Battle Theme
Tempo: 105 BPM, 6/8 Slow Pulsing Compound Meter, Key of C# Phrygian / Hirajōshi Scale
Instrumentation: Metallic iron chain scrapes and strikes acting as chilling percussion, deep subsonic sub-bass frequencies resonant in stone caverns, ritualistic Buddhist tantric chanting drones, dissonant bent-note Koto and Kokyū (bowed lute), hollow bamboo Shakuhachi with extended breath techniques, ritual bronze bells (rin) with shimmering decay.
Structure & Dynamics:
- Intro (0:00 - 0:16): Wet cave water drops echo in the dark; metallic iron chains clink and slide; low guttural chanting begins over an eerie amethyst drone.
- Occult Pulse (0:16 - 0:55): A hypnotic, ritualistic 6/8 rhythm takes form with muted ritual drums and resonant stone clacks; weeping kokyū melodies weave around sinister koto arpeggios in the Japanese Hirajōshi scale.
- Spectral Climax (0:55 - 1:20): Dark magical surge with rising vocal chants, aggressive metallic chain strikes, echoing bronze bell hits, and swirling ethereal sound design mimicking shadowy apparitions.
- Seamless Loop Tail: The spectral chants dissolve into echoing cave water drops and reverberating bronze bells, looping seamlessly.
Mix & Texture: Deep spatial reverb creating immense cavernous depth; eerie stereo imaging with scraping metal and whispered spells; authentic 90s Neo-Geo dark fantasy arcade vibe.
```

#### 📜 Descrição Conceitual (Português):
* **Foco Rítmico**: Pulso ritualístico em compasso 6/8 com percussão metálica de correntes raspando e batidas pesadas em pedra.
* **Arranjo Melódico**: Kokyū (alaúde com arco) e Koto em escala Hirajōshi misteriosa, acompanhados por canto coral budista gutural e ressonância de sinos tibetanos/tenta.

## 📌 Guia de Integração no Jogo
Quando as faixas forem geradas em áudio (.ogg / .mp3):
1. Normalizar o áudio para **-14 LUFS** com pico máximo em **-1.0 dB True Peak**.
2. Criar ponto de loop sem clique (*loop point metadata* ou arquivo cortado exatamente no compasso de volta).
3. Salvar em `assets/audio/music/`:
   - `bgm_shaolin_temple.ogg`
   - `bgm_higanbana_garden.ogg`
   - `bgm_nagashino_field.ogg`
   - `bgm_shadow_cave.ogg`
4. O `SoundManager` já está configurado para mapear os IDs de arena diretamente para essas faixas de BGM via `spec.music`.
